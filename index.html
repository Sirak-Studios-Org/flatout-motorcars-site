Engineering

Weight is the enemy. We removed it.

The rest of the market keeps adding power. The Drakan wins by taking weight out of the formula, then engineering everything around the driver.

The chassis

A tubular chromoly steel chassis, over-engineered to be light and to keep the driver and passenger protected inside a racing cage.

Formula suspension

Pushrod and bell-crank suspension geometry taken straight from motorsport. It is the difference you feel the moment you turn in.

Serviceable by design

Body panels release with a single push and parts are chosen to be sourceable anywhere. Ownership stays simple.

Made by a driver

Built on 37 years behind the wheel

Flatout Motor Cars was founded by Albert Gocsman, a lifelong racing driver who built the Drakan out of decades of seat time.

The knowledge is real and it is in the car. Albert has spent his life chasing the purest way to drive fast, and the Drakan is that answer made street legal. Every build carries the same intent: provide a true driving experience while keeping the driver safe.
