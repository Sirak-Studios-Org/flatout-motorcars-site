The engineering

Weight is the enemy. We removed it.

The rest of the market keeps adding power. The Drakan wins by taking weight out of the formula, then engineering everything around the driver.

The chassis

A tubular chromoly steel chassis, over-engineered to be light and to keep the driver and passenger protected inside a racing cage. It is the backbone that lets the car hit roughly 2,000 pounds without giving up strength.

Formula One geometry

Pushrod and bell-crank suspension with Formula One geometry, engineered to be road-compliant. It is the difference you feel the moment you turn in: a true race-car response that stays composed on public roads.

Serviceable by design

Body panels release with a single push and parts are chosen to be sourceable anywhere. The Drakan is a real car for real roads, and ownership stays simple wherever you drive it.

Good to know

Specification questions

How much does the Flatout Drakan weigh?

The Drakan weighs roughly 2,000 pounds wet, built around a tubular chromoly chassis engineered to be light and to protect the driver and passenger inside a racing cage.

What engine and transmission does the Drakan use?

The Drakan is powered by an LS3 6.2L V8 paired with a Getrag 6-speed manual and a bespoke racecar pedal box.

How fast is the Flatout Drakan?

The Drakan reaches 0 to 60 mph in 2.8 seconds and tops out at 175 mph, at roughly 2,000 pounds wet.

What suspension does the Drakan use?

The Drakan uses a pushrod and bell-crank suspension with Formula One geometry, engineered to be road-compliant while delivering a true race-car feel.

Is the Flatout Drakan street legal?

Yes. The Drakan is street legal and plate-ready, and it is serviceable anywhere, with body panels that release with a single push and parts chosen to be sourceable anywhere.
